Before you call.

How often should air-conditioning units be serviced?
For commercial premises we recommend monthly preventative maintenance, which is what our commercial contracts are built around. Residential units benefit from servicing at least twice a year, more often in dusty locations or where units run continuously.
Do you supply the units as well as install them?
Yes. We supply air-conditioning units and genuine spare parts at competitive rates, and we can advise on the right brand and capacity for your environment. Supply-only is also available if you have your own installer.
Can you handle the electrical work too?
Yes. Electrical installations and maintenance are a core service line, so the power supply, isolation and controls for your HVAC equipment are handled by the same team, to the same standards.
Do you work outside Accra?
We are based in McCarthy Hill, Accra and serve clients across Ghana and beyond. Contact us with the location and scope of the work and we will confirm availability.
Will I get documentation for the work?
Yes. We maintain comprehensive service records including installation, commissioning, servicing and repair documentation, and we provide recommendations to enhance system performance.
