01 / Sales & installations

Customised HVAC Design & Installation

We supply and install high-quality air conditioning and electrical systems at competitive rates. Whether you are installing a new system or upgrading an existing one, we provide affordable and efficient solutions that maximise your investment.

Our team provides the guidance you need in sourcing and choosing unit types and brands for specific environments, then handles installation with the care your building deserves.

  • Site assessment and system sizing for the actual space and occupancy
  • Guidance on unit types, capacities and brands
  • Split, multi-split, ceiling cassette, ducted and VRF systems
  • Commissioning and handover documentation
02 / Service & maintenance

Preventative Maintenance & Servicing

Get exceptional servicing of your units, affordable repairs and comprehensive solutions to ensure your home, office or property remains comfortable and energy efficient.

Our team delivers cost-effective repairs and proactive servicing to extend the lifespan of your equipment. Units are serviced under strict industry directives, prolonging their life and keeping running costs down.

  • Monthly preventative maintenance plans for commercial clients
  • Filter, coil and drainage cleaning; refrigerant and electrical checks
  • Performance verification against manufacturer specification
  • A written service record after every visit
03 / Repairs

HVAC Repairs & System Expansions

When a unit fails, we diagnose the root cause and repair it properly: supply and replacement of damaged parts, not just a reset. And when your building or team grows, we expand the system to match.

  • Fault diagnosis: refrigerant, electrical, controls and mechanical
  • Repair, supply and replacement of damaged parts
  • System expansions for new floors, rooms and occupancy
  • Recommendations to enhance system performance
04 / Electrical

Electrical Installations & Maintenance

Air conditioning is only as reliable as the power behind it. We install and maintain the electrical systems that serve HVAC equipment and the wider building, in line with safety standards and statutory requirements.

  • Power supply, distribution and isolation for HVAC equipment
  • Wiring, control circuits and protection devices
  • Electrical maintenance alongside your HVAC service schedule
  • Compliance with all relevant industry regulations
05 / Supply

Supply of HVAC Units & Parts

We supply quality air-conditioning units and genuine spare parts at competitive rates, with the option of professional installation by the same team. Our vast experience handling unit brands of different sizes and capacities means you get the right equipment for your environment.

  • Air-conditioning units for residential, commercial and industrial use
  • Compressors, fans, motors, filters, controls and consumables
  • Advice on brand and capacity selection
  • Supply-only or supply-and-install

Common questions

Before you call.

Quick answers to what clients usually ask first. Anything else, send us a message and we will respond.

How often should air-conditioning units be serviced?
For commercial premises we recommend monthly preventative maintenance, which is what our commercial contracts are built around. Residential units benefit from servicing at least twice a year, more often in dusty locations or where units run continuously.
Do you supply the units as well as install them?
Yes. We supply air-conditioning units and genuine spare parts at competitive rates, and we can advise on the right brand and capacity for your environment. Supply-only is also available if you have your own installer.
Can you handle the electrical work too?
Yes. Electrical installations and maintenance are a core service line, so the power supply, isolation and controls for your HVAC equipment are handled by the same team, to the same standards.
Do you work outside Accra?
We are based in McCarthy Hill, Accra and serve clients across Ghana and beyond. Contact us with the location and scope of the work and we will confirm availability.
Will I get documentation for the work?
Yes. We maintain comprehensive service records including installation, commissioning, servicing and repair documentation, and we provide recommendations to enhance system performance.
