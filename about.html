About us

Nearly a decade of keeping Ghana comfortable.

HVAC Technical Services is a proudly indigenous Ghanaian company, based in Accra, delivering high-quality, cost-effective HVAC and electrical solutions to clients across Ghana and beyond.

OHS Team trained to Occupational Health and Safety standards

A word from our Technical Director

Comfort, efficiency and quality your business deserves.

HVAC Technical Services is a proudly indigenous Ghanaian company with nearly a decade of expertise in designing, installing and maintaining heating, ventilation, air conditioning and electrical systems. Based in Accra, we take pride in delivering high-quality, cost-effective solutions to clients across Ghana and beyond.

We serve a diverse range of industries, including restaurants, manufacturing plants, office environments, apartment complexes, retail properties and shopping malls. Whether it is a new system installation, routine maintenance or electrical services, we ensure efficiency, reliability and safety in every project we undertake.

We are committed to excellence. Our team is trained to comply with Occupational Health and Safety standards, ensuring top-tier workmanship and service delivery. Let us provide the comfort, efficiency and quality your business deserves. We look forward to the opportunity to serve you.

Mohammed Abdulai Technical Director, HVAC Technical Services

Company overview

What we bring to every project.

Since our inception, commercial and residential HVAC installation, maintenance and electrical services have been at the core of our business.

We specialise in managing, servicing and installing HVAC and electrical systems. Our highly skilled workforce is equipped with the expertise, safety knowledge and advanced tools needed to deliver exceptional service, ensuring efficiency, reliability and compliance with the highest standards.
