How a project runs

From first assessment to the final service record.

Custom specifications, clear timelines and agreed budgets. We document every installation, commissioning, service and repair so you always know the state of your equipment.

  1. Step 01

    Site assessment

    We visit, measure and understand the space, the occupancy and the power available before recommending anything.

  2. Step 02

    Design & proposal

    Guidance on unit types, capacities and brands for your environment, with a clear specification, timeline and budget.

  3. Step 03

    Installation & commissioning

    Installed by OHS-trained technicians to safety and environmental standards, then commissioned and handed over with documentation.

  4. Step 04

    Maintenance & records

    Scheduled preventative maintenance, prompt repairs and a full service history with recommendations to improve performance.
