    What the number above cannot see.

    A figure from a form gets you in the right range, which is enough to budget and to rule options in or out. Before we quote, we come and check the things that move it.

    The fabric

    Ceiling height, insulation, how many walls face outside, what is above and below the room, and how well the doors and windows actually seal.

    The load inside

    Equipment, lighting, cooking, how long the room is occupied and how often the door opens. A busy shop and a quiet office of the same size are not the same job.

    The install

    Where the outdoor unit can go, pipe run length, drainage, and whether the supply can carry it. These decide what is practical, not just what is adequate.
